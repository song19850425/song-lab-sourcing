What we source

Two categories, both specified and checked by people who have run sampling and water-testing programs themselves: the gear field teams use on site, and the instruments that turn samples into results.

Category 01

Sampling equipment & consumables

Our first category — everything a field team needs to collect a defensible sample and get it to the lab intact.

Water samplers

Depth samplers, bailers, and peristaltic pumps for surface water, groundwater, and wastewater sampling.

Soil samplers

Augers, core samplers, and ring-knife kits for environmental soil investigation and remediation work.

Sample bottles & containers

Glass and PE bottles in the sizes and materials your methods require — amber glass, sterile, pre-preserved.

Preservation kits & reagents

Acid and alkali preservation kits matched to the analyte — so samples survive the trip to the lab.

Field consumables

Cool boxes, labels, filters, gloves, and the small items that decide whether a sampling day succeeds.

Gas sampling bags & canisters

Tedlar and foil gas sampling bags, plus canisters for air and stack-gas sampling programs.

Category 02

Water quality testing instruments

Our second category — field and benchtop instruments for water and wastewater testing. Instruments are not consumables: range, accuracy, calibration, and reagents decide whether the numbers hold up, so every model is checked on specs before it is quoted.

Portable multiparameter meters

Handheld meters for pH, dissolved oxygen, conductivity, ORP, and temperature — checked for sensor type, calibration points, and replaceable electrodes.

Turbidity meters

Portable and benchtop turbidity meters for drinking water, surface water, and effluent — verified on measuring range, light source, and calibration standards supplied.

COD analyzers & digestion reactors

COD measurement by spectrophotometric methods, with matching digestion reactors — checked for digestion temperature and time, vial compatibility, and reagent supply.

Ammonia & nutrient analyzers

Colorimetric analyzers and test kits for ammonia nitrogen, total phosphorus, and total nitrogen — matched to the method and range your discharge limits require.

BOD analyzers & incubators

BOD5 measurement systems and thermostatic incubators — checked for bottle volume, stirring, temperature stability, and the 5-day test workflow.

Spectrophotometers, reagents & standards

UV-Vis and visible spectrophotometers, plus the reagents, cuvettes, and calibration standards they run on — so the instrument arrives ready to work, not waiting on consumables.

What we check before an instrument is quoted

  • Range & accuracy — against your method and your limits, not the brochure headline.
  • Calibration — standards included, calibration points, and what a calibration certificate actually covers.
  • Reagents & spares — reagent compatibility and shelf life, spare electrodes and sensors, and reordering lead time.
  • Fit for your lab — voltage and plug, English manual and interface, and warranty and after-sales terms in writing.

Can you source water quality testing instruments, not just consumables?

Yes — that is our second category: portable meters, turbidity meters, COD and nutrient analyzers, BOD systems, and spectrophotometers. Send the parameters you test and the ranges you need; we match models to your method instead of pushing whatever a factory wants to move.
